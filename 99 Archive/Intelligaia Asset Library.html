The Intelligaia mechanism

Construction process.

Every finished illustration in this library is built in stages on the same DP grid. The wireframe and deconstructed views below are not styles — they are the making-of. Click any asset card to see its own construction sequence.

01 · Grid
DP grid

Every illustration starts on the 50×50 dot-pixel grid. Establishes proportion, rhythm and modularity before a single shape is drawn.

02 · Deconstructed
Primitives

Rectangles, circles and semi-rectangles layered on the grid. The geometric skeleton of the illustration — pure construction, no fill.

03 · Outlined
Wireframe

The primitives merge into a single line-only wireframe. Silhouette locked, proportions verified — this is the blueprint, not a delivered style.

04 · Final
Final fill

Colour, weight and character from the Intelligaia palette. The only stage that ships to product — the one the audience sees.
