System
A camera-based examiner that scores the driving-licence box test - objective, evidenced and auditable.
The challenge

Turn a by-eye judgment on a few centimetres of rubber into a measured, defensible result - the same for every candidate.

System · Live box-test evaluation, with the tyre-line touch caught to the millimetre.
The solution

Ground-plane calibration, so a touch is measured against the real painted line in millimetres - not inferred from pixels at an angle.

Two cameras fused with tyre-ground segmentation, so a wheel hidden in one view is caught by the other.

Tamper-evident evidence and a manual-review fallback, so every verdict is trusted and appealable.

*Figures describe the system's design, not guaranteed performance. Based on the current pilot build; on-site field validation is ongoing.
