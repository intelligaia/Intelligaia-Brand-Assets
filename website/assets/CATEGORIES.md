# Asset category rules

All 496 SVGs were visually reviewed on 2026-11-20. Category follows the depicted subject. A secondary use case belongs in tags rather than a duplicate copy. SVG contents are unchanged.

## Placement rules

- Nature contains flora, fauna, natural clouds, landscapes and weather. Food, vehicles, lighting equipment and computing-cloud symbols do not belong here.
- Devices contains computer, mobile, audio, wearable, printing, component and kiosk hardware. Cameras and optical equipment remain under cameras-optics.
- Vehicles contains road and rail transport. Aircraft stay in aviation-space, vessels in maritime-nautical, and construction equipment in machinery-industry.
- Robotics-automation contains service robots, robotic arms and automated machinery. A charging station belongs in energy-systems, not Robotics.
- Furniture-storage contains bare furniture. Complete furnished workstations and meeting setups belong in business-workplace; retail display fixtures remain in commerce-retail.
- Tools contains hand tools and tool storage. Bulbs and flashlights belong in lighting; clocks and calendars belong in time-scheduling.
- Networking-infrastructure contains network hardware, topology and cloud-computing symbols. A firewall belongs in security-trust.
- Icons are grouped by semantic purpose: accounts, scheduling, help, alerts, verification, global reach, analytics, interface navigation, strategy, AI and abstract decoration.
- Numbers and letters are named for the actual glyph. Patterns holds decorative textures.
- CX Roadmap thumbnails remain a coherent workflow collection, including research personas and design/development deliverables.
