Philosophy.

Design is a tool, not a taste.

It exists to make the person on the other side more capable — never to impress the person making it. That sentence is the tiebreaker for every decision in this system, and it applies equally to a designer in Figma and an agent generating a page at three in the morning.

It settles argumentsWhen two options both look fine, the one that makes the reader more capable wins. Taste is not a tiebreaker.
It binds agents tooOutput that flatters the brand but confuses the reader has failed, whoever or whatever produced it.
It is falsifiableYou can point at an element and say it serves nobody. A principle you cannot fail is decoration.

Three principles.

In priority order. When two conflict, resolve toward the one listed first.

Goodness leads.

Every decision passes one test: does it make the person on the other side more capable, better informed, or more at ease? If the answer needs justifying with brand, ego or novelty, remove it and try again.

Do
  • Choose clarity over cleverness in every headline
  • Show progress and current state in every flow
  • Let content set the layout, not the reverse
Don't
  • Add motion or texture that doesn't serve comprehension
  • Hide primary actions behind aesthetic choices
  • Optimise for portfolio screenshots

Intent-aware.

Interfaces are the sum of user intents. Every element on screen must serve the intent the user has right now — not one they might have later. Postpone. Remove. When in doubt, cut.

Do
  • Surface the next best action for the current context
  • Use progressive disclosure for advanced capability
  • Reveal density on demand, not by default
Don't
  • Populate dashboards with every metric that exists
  • Treat empty states as blank space
  • Add tabs to postpone hard content decisions

Human-centered.

Agents write, generate and propose. Humans review, edit and ship. Interfaces stay legible and reversible, automated actions are labelled as such, and consent is explicit rather than buried.

Do
  • Label AI-generated content clearly and consistently
  • Provide a way to undo any automated action
  • Show sources, confidence and provenance
Don't
  • Auto-apply agent changes without human review
  • Hide attribution to feel more magical
  • Present opaque confidence as certainty

Four colours.

Yellow is the signature. Ink is the surface. Blue and purple are functional accents — never both in one viewport. Flat fills only; no gradient touches the yellow.

Signature yellow#FFC800 · --yellowHero banners, primary CTAs, highlights, key numbers.
Ink#04050A · --inkDark surface; body type on the light theme.
Trust blue#4A8BF5 · --blueData visualisation, information states, links.
Insight purple#8A5AF0 · --purpleAI, agents, moments of insight. Used sparingly.
