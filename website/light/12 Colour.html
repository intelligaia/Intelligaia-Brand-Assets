Rules

Do & don't.

Colour discipline is the single largest lever on brand recognition. Follow these rules on every page.

Do

  • Use flat colour — no gradients on the yellow, ever
  • One accent per viewport (either Blue or Purple, never both)
  • Reserve Yellow for the single most important surface on the page
  • Test contrast: minimum 4.5:1 for body type, 3:1 for large display

Don't

  • Introduce any colour outside the four brand hues + neutrals
  • Use Yellow as a background for body-length text
  • Combine Blue and Purple in the same block or chart
  • Add gradients, overlays or transparency effects to hero banners
