Social media

One idea, three seconds.

Square, portrait and story formats — the anatomy of a post that reads as Intelligaia from the thumbnail.

Purpose

One idea, three seconds.

A social post is the smallest unit of Intelligaia communication. It carries one idea, holds it for three seconds in a hostile feed, and looks unmistakably ours from the thumbnail alone. The yellow does most of that work.

Idea

Exactly one

If it needs two, it's a carousel or two posts.

Words

12 maximum

On the image itself. Detail belongs in the caption.

Recognition

Yellow first

Signature colour visible at thumbnail size.

Mark

Always present

Logo bottom-left or bottom-right. Never centred.

Formats

Three ratios, no others.

Design once at the largest ratio, then crop deliberately — never let a platform auto-crop the headline.

Square

1080 × 1080

LinkedIn · Instagram feed

Portrait

1080 × 1350

Instagram · best feed real estate

Story

1080 × 1920

Stories · Reels cover
Anatomy

What goes on the canvas.

Background

Flat yellow, flat ink, or one approved illustration. Never a gradient, never a photo.

Layer 1
Headline

Oswald 500. Largest element on the canvas. Maximum 12 words, ideally 6.

Layer 2
Support line

Optional. Oswald 400 at roughly half the headline size. One line only.

Layer 3
Illustration

One asset from the library, anchored to a corner or edge. Never centred behind text.

Layer 4
Logo mark

Bottom-left or bottom-right, 48px clear space. Consistent across a campaign.

Layer 5
Rules

Do & don't.

Do

  • Test legibility at 120px wide — thumbnail size
  • Keep the headline clear of the bottom 15% (UI overlap)
  • Reuse one illustration across a campaign for recognition
  • Put the nuance in the caption, not on the image

Don't

  • No more than 12 words on the canvas
  • No stock photography or AI-generated imagery
  • No text over a busy area of an illustration
  • No platform logos or engagement-bait graphics
For agents

Prompt an agent can run.

Replace the bracketed inputs and hand this to an agent with system and manifest access.

Agent prompt
Produce an Intelligaia social post for [TOPIC / ANNOUNCEMENT].

Follow the Social Media playbook at:  80 Social Media.html

Inputs required:
  - headline        (max 12 words, ideally 6)
  - support_line    (optional, one line)
  - format          (square 1080x1080 | portrait 1080x1350 | story 1080x1920)
  - illustration    (path from assets/manifest.json)
  - caption         (the nuance goes here, not on the image)

Constraints:
  - ONE idea per post
  - Background: flat #FFC800, flat #04050A, or one approved illustration — no gradients, no photos
  - Headline Oswald 500, largest element; support line Oswald 400 at ~half size
  - Logo bottom-left or bottom-right, 48px clear space
  - Headline clear of bottom 15% (platform UI overlap)
  - Must stay legible at 120px wide

Output: SVG or HTML canvas at the exact pixel ratio + the caption text separately.
