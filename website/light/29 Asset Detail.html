How it is made.

The illustration is not drawn freehand. It is assembled from primitives on a 50×50 dot grid, then resolved. Only the last stage ships — the others are process, not style.

The method.

Rather than drawing the subject, we place a grid, lay geometric primitives on it, then intersect and merge them until the silhouette appears. That constraint is what makes an Intelligaia illustration recognisable.

Grid firstEvery illustration starts on the 50×50 dot grid. Snap is always on — if an anchor misses an intersection, delete it and try again.
Four primitivesStandard angles only — 0°, 15°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 75°, 90°. Curves are quarter, half or full circles.
Intersect, don't drawThe silhouette emerges where primitives overlap and merge. Remove what does not earn its place.
