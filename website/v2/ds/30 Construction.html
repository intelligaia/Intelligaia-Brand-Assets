Construction method.

Illustrations are assembled from four primitives on a fixed grid. This is what keeps 279 assets looking like one hand made them.

How it is built
Grid

Everything starts on the 50×50 grid. Proportion is decided before any shape is placed.

Primitives

Four shapes only. Circle, semicircle, rectangle, and the semi-rectangle — a rectangle with one rounded side.

Intersect

Form comes from overlap, not outline. Shapes are layered until the silhouette appears.

Ship

Only the final stage leaves the building. Geometry and outline are working stages.

The four primitives

Circle

Heads, eyes, lenses, joints. Carries most of the warmth in a figure.

Semicircle

Hair, shoulders, arches. Direction comes from which half is kept.

Rectangle

Torsos, screens, structure. Keeps a composition from floating.

Semi-rectangle

One rounded side. Limbs and cuffs, where a hard edge would read as cold.

Three stages
Working

Geometry

Primitives layered on the grid, still visibly separate. The cheapest place to be wrong about proportion.

Working

Outline

The primitives merge into one wireframe. Proportion verified, composition committed.

Ships

Final

Shading and detail resolved. The delivered asset, and the only download in the manifest.

Where to see it. Open any character in the asset library — each detail page shows its three stages against the finished illustration.
