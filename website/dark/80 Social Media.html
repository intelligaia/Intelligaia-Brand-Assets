Social media

Social media.

Three ratios, five layers, twelve words maximum.

The output

Three formats.

One idea, three seconds, a hostile feed. The yellow does most of the recognition work before anyone reads a word.

Square1080 × 1080 · LinkedIn, IG feed
Portrait1080 × 1350 · best feed space
Story1080 × 1920 · stories, reels
Deconstructed

Five layers on the canvas.

BackgroundFlat yellow, flat ink, or one approved illustration. Never a photo.
HeadlineOswald 400. Largest element. Twelve words maximum, six is better.
Support lineOptional. Oswald 400 at roughly half the headline size.
IllustrationOne library asset, anchored to a corner. Never behind the text.
Logo markBottom-left or bottom-right, 48px clear space.
Safe areaKeep the headline clear of the bottom 15% — platform UI sits there.
Build it with AI

Hand this to an agent.

Prompt
Produce an Intelligaia social post for [TOPIC].

Playbook: 80 Social Media.html

Inputs:
  headline       max 12 words, ideally 6
  support_line   optional, one line
  format         square 1080x1080 | portrait 1080x1350 | story 1080x1920
  illustration   path from assets/manifest.json
  caption        the nuance goes here, not on the image

Layers: background → headline → support → illustration → logo

Output: SVG or HTML canvas at exact pixel ratio, plus the caption separately.

Must

  • Legible at 120px wide — thumbnail size
  • Headline clear of the bottom 15%
  • One illustration reused across a campaign
  • Nuance in the caption, not on the image

Never

  • More than twelve words on the canvas
  • Stock or AI-generated imagery
  • Text over a busy area of an illustration
  • Platform logos or engagement bait
