Foundations · Type

Two families. One rhythm.

Oswald carries the display voice. Geist carries the reading load. Nothing else.

Display · Oswald
Aa
Display · condensed sans

Oswald.

Every headline, hero H1, section title. Condensed proportions carry authority at large sizes without shouting. Two weights only — Medium (500) for signature headlines and section titles, Regular (400) for sub-heads and quiet display. No light, no bold.

400 · Regular 500 · Medium
Body · Geist
Aa
Body · neutral sans

Geist.

Everything that isn't a headline — body copy, ledes, descriptions, labels, buttons, nav, tags — plus monospace (Geist Mono). Neutral geometry, honest metrics, designed for interfaces. Running text stays at 400; 500/600 is permitted only for micro-labels under 13px. Never italic.

400 · Regular 500 · Micro-labels Geist Mono · 400
Type scale

Seven steps. Nothing between.

The full type scale — pick from these seven steps, never invent an intermediate size.

D1 · Hero Design that earns trust. Oswald 500 · 96px / 0.98
D2 · Big Section headline. Oswald 500 · 64px / 1.05
H1 Page heading. Oswald 500 · 40px / 1.1
H2 Sub-section. Oswald 500 · 28px / 1.15
Body L Lede paragraphs and article intros. Long-form reading size. Geist 400 · 18px / 1.55
Body Default running text — descriptions, captions, metadata, labels. Geist 400 · 15px / 1.6
Meta TAGS · TIMESTAMPS · FOOTNOTES Geist 500 · 12px / 0.06em tracking
Rules

Do & don't.

Do

  • Set headlines in Oswald Medium (500) or Regular (400) with tight tracking (-0.015em)
  • Use Geist 400 for all running text — 500 only for micro-labels under 13px
  • Match line-height to context: 0.98 for display, 1.55 for reading
  • Cap line length at 62 characters for reading

Don't

  • Introduce a third type family — Oswald and Geist (+ Geist Mono) are the whole system
  • Use Oswald below 24px, or at any weight other than 400 / 500
  • Bold or italicise Geist — pick a heavier weight instead (there isn't one)
  • Mix uppercase and title case in the same headline
