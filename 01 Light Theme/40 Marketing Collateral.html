The System

How this catalog is made.

Every page in the catalog is built to the same rules. Toggle the golden-rule grid above to see the underlying construction.

Grid

12-column layout, 4pt base.

Every page is laid out on a 12-column grid with a 4pt vertical rhythm. Nothing sits off-grid.

Typography

Oswald + Geist.

Oswald Medium for headings. Geist Regular for body copy. Seven-step scale — no other weights.

Colour

30 brand tokens.

Every colour drawn from the locked palette — brand, primary, secondary, base, skin, hair groups.

Illustrations

Three-primitive language.

Every illustration uses the Illustration System's three primitives and follows the three-stage build.

Anatomy

Cover · About · Capabilities · Case study.

Fixed opening sequence. Additions plug in as new case-study pages.

Editable slots

What can change.

Copy, hero illustration, case-study content, metric callouts are editable. Grid, typography, colour and anatomy are locked.
