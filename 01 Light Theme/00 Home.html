Guiding Principles

Principles & philosophy.

Three commitments that anchor every Intelligaia design decision — and the philosophy behind the new brand.

Open principles

Goodness leads. Intent-aware. Human-centered. These are the operating instructions for the design team — before a colour is picked, a shape is drawn or a screen is laid out.
