# Cisco Commerce Workspace (CCW) — Case-Study Knowledge

## Historical problem context
The supplied case study describes a fragmented CPQ experience involving multiple tools, manual tracking, limited approval-status visibility, lengthy quoting, support burden, learning curve, and an inconsistent/error-prone experience.

## Transformation approach
Unified Experience + User First Approach + Definition of Design Language; persona/journey work; information architecture; iterative sketch/prototype cycles; high-fidelity designs; design system/component library; design through development.

## Case-study outcome
CCW is described as a combined experience for quoting, configuring, and ordering Cisco products, software, and services.

## Attributed historical metrics
The case study reports 3x faster deal cycle, 5x more quotes/partner-productivity framing, and 85% of customer interactions powered by AI chatbot. Preserve attribution; do not use as generic benchmarks.
