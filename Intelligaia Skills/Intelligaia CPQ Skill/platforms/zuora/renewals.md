# Zuora — Renewal Project Findings

## Confirmed project pain points
Research/assessment material describes manual renewal work, duplicate/manual opportunity activity, difficulty seeing historical pricing/discounts, third-party renewal-alert dependency, add-on/amendment complexity, and order/quote reconciliation issues.

## Proposed future-state themes
Business-rule-driven renewal opportunity and quote creation; expiring/evergreen subscription logic; purchase-history visibility; quantity expansion/upgrades; standard new-business/renewal quote-to-order process; renewal integration.

## Historical product-gap note
The assessment classified renewal opportunity auto-update and renewal quote automation as requiring customization in the assessed CPQx state. Treat this as historical, not current product truth.
