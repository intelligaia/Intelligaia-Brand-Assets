# Partner Sales — Controls, Data & Governance

## 1. Party-Role Model
Explicitly distinguish relevant roles:
partner/reseller, distributor, end customer, sold-to, bill-to, payer, ship-to, service-to, end user, opportunity owner and vendor seller.

Do not assume one account performs all roles.

## 2. Partner Authorization
Define who may sell what, where, to whom, through which channel, during which period, and under which program/contract.

## 3. End-Customer Integrity
Preserve the true consuming/contracted customer according to the business model even when another party transacts or pays.

## 4. Deal Registration
Where used, define submission, uniqueness/conflict check, approval, validity, ownership/protection and expiry.

## 5. Channel Eligibility
A product may have distinct direct, partner, distributor, geography or program eligibility.

## 6. Pricing Context
Pricing must use the correct partner, customer, channel, geography, currency, date, quantity, term and contract/program context.

## 7. Price Layers
Where applicable distinguish:
reference/list price → partner buy/acquisition price → partner/customer discount → end-customer sell/net price → vendor recognized amount.

Actual layers require evidence.

## 8. Price Visibility
Define which commercial values are visible to vendor seller, partner, distributor and end customer.

## 9. Margin
If margin is used, define numerator, denominator, price basis, included incentives and rounding. Do not assume markup and margin are interchangeable.

## 10. Partner Discount / Incentive
Separate contractual/program entitlement from discretionary special pricing.

## 11. Customer Discount
Define whether partner may independently set customer sell price and what vendor controls apply.

## 12. Special Pricing
Preserve request, reason, requested/approved values, approver, validity period, products/customer and exact quote/version context.

## 13. Approval Integrity
Approval should evaluate the correct economic layer and remain tied to the version reviewed.

## 14. Quote / Document Visibility
Customer-facing output must not unintentionally expose partner-confidential economics, and partner-facing output must follow approved visibility rules.

## 15. Party Mapping to Order
Define:
**Quote Party Role → Transformation → Order Party Role → Downstream Customer/Account Role → Validation**

## 16. Attribution
Preserve partner/channel attribution across opportunity, quote, order, subscription and renewal according to policy.

## 17. Multi-Tier Channel
Where distributor/intermediary exists, preserve each transaction/party relationship rather than flattening it into a single partner.

## 18. Billing Relationship
Explicitly define who is billed, who pays, whose tax/billing attributes apply, and how end-customer identity is retained.

## 19. Installed-Base Ownership
Define whether subscription/asset belongs to end customer, partner, payer or another party in each business model. Do not infer.

## 20. Renewal Ownership
Define who owns/initiates renewal and whether the original partner retains rights.

## 21. Partner-of-Record Change
Define eligibility, effective timing, historical preservation, future attribution and impact on open transactions.

## 22. Channel Conflict
Define evidence and decision authority for competing partner claims.

## 23. Duplicate Prevention
Deal registration, quote, order and distributor retries should not create unintended duplicate transactions.

## 24. Audit
Preserve partner/customer identities, authorization, pricing basis, discounts/special pricing, approvals, attribution changes and downstream references.

## 25. Reconciliation
Compare approved party roles and economics to order, subscription, billing and attribution outcomes.

## Risks
Potential hypotheses:
wrong partner authorization; end customer missing; partner price shown to customer; customer price used as vendor buy price; margin miscalculated; special pricing reused; distributor chain lost; vendor bills wrong party; installed base assigned to partner instead of end customer; renewal partner attribution incorrect; channel conflict resolved manually without audit.

Validate before asserting.
