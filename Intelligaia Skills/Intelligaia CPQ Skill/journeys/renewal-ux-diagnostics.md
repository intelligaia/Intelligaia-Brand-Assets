# Renewal Journey — UX, Diagnostics & KPIs

## Renewal Seller Experience
A strong renewal workspace should answer:
- Which customers/subscriptions require action?
- When is the renewal boundary?
- What does the customer have now?
- What will be effective at renewal?
- Which products can renew as-is?
- Which require migration?
- What changed since the prior term?
- What is the proposed price/uplift and why?
- Which discounts/approvals apply?
- What is the customer decision and next action?

## Customer Success Experience
Where appropriate, expose renewal timing, installed-base state, pending issues, product changes, expansion/contraction signals and customer decision without requiring reconstruction across systems.

## Prior vs Proposed Pattern
Prefer:
**Prior Term | Renewal Baseline | Proposed Renewal | Accepted Renewal**

This makes carry-forward, migration, expansion, contraction and repricing visible.

## Renewal Timeline
**Renewal Trigger → Readiness → Quote → Customer Engagement → Decision → Order → Prior-Term End → Next-Term Start**

## Diagnostic Questions
- Are renewals discovered through spreadsheets?
- Are renewal transactions duplicated?
- Is baseline state current?
- Are pending amendments reflected?
- Can sellers explain uplift/repricing?
- Are obsolete products identified before proposal?
- Are discounts copied without governance?
- Are partial renewals easy to represent?
- Are non-renewal decisions propagated to billing/entitlements?
- Are next-term charges aligned to the accepted quote?

## Renewal Journey Diagnostic
**Trigger → Readiness → Baseline → Carry-Forward/Migration → Pricing/Uplift → Discount → Approval → Quote → Negotiation → Decision → Order → Next Term → Billing/Entitlement → Evidence**

## Readiness Diagnostic
**Required Input/Condition → Available? → Source → Quality → Owner → Blocker → Remediation → Evidence**

## Commercial Diagnostic
**Prior Value → Pricing Basis → Uplift/Repricing → Discount → Proposed Value → Accepted Value → Difference/Reason → Evidence**

## Migration Diagnostic
**Source Product → Renewability → Target Product → Configuration/Quantity Mapping → Commercial Effect → Accepted Result → Evidence**

## Churn Diagnostic
**Renewal Candidate → Customer Decision → Non-Renewed Scope → End Date → Billing Stop → Entitlement End → Pipeline Update → Evidence**

## Common Failure Patterns
Treat as hypotheses:
spreadsheet renewal calendar; stale quote used as baseline; pending amendment missed; manual uplift; expired SKU renewed; wrong quantity; uncontrolled discount carry-forward; duplicate renewal opportunity/quote; non-renewal does not stop billing; entitlement remains active; next-term gap/overlap; operations cannot trace predecessor/successor.

## KPI Examples
Renewal readiness; renewal quote lead time; cycle time; manual touches; duplicate renewals; baseline corrections; migration exceptions; uplift exceptions; quote revisions; approval turnaround; on-time renewal completion; non-renewal processing errors; reconciliation exceptions.

GRR, NRR, renewal rate and churn definitions must follow the customer's approved metric definitions.

## Opportunity Patterns
Renewal workbench, readiness indicators, pending-change awareness, prior-vs-proposed comparison, uplift explanation, migration guidance, renewal timeline, non-renewal workflow and next-term reconciliation.
