# Intelligaia Opportunity / Service Mapping

## Purpose
Translate evidenced CPQ/Q2C findings into appropriate Intelligaia service opportunities without turning hypotheses into customer facts or fabricating quantified value.

## Core Rule
**Evidence → Finding → Business Consequence → Capability/Experience Gap → Service Opportunity → Expected Outcome → Validation Measure**

Do not start with a service and reverse-engineer a customer problem.

## Evidence States
Use the source evidence state:
Validated · Historical · Hypothesis · Proposed · Unknown / Validation Needed.

## Sales Guardrail
A service opportunity may be recommended when the evidence supports the underlying problem or discovery need. It must not be presented as a confirmed customer pain point when the evidence is only hypothetical or incomplete.

## Value Guardrail
Use qualitative outcomes unless a quantified baseline, target, or measured result is supported by evidence.
