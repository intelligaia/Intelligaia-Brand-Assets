# Product Engineering & Modernization Opportunities

## Use When
Evidence supports a capability, maintainability, extensibility, reliability or implementation gap that requires software/product engineering.

## Typical Evidence Signals
- required CPQ behavior not supported by evidenced current solution;
- brittle custom workflow or duplicated logic;
- manual orchestration caused by missing capability;
- inconsistent rule execution;
- unsupported/legacy implementation;
- repeated defects around state/version handling;
- inability to support required channel/lifecycle scenario;
- validated UX requiring implementation.

## Service Motions
### CPQ Capability Engineering
Configuration, pricing, quoting, approval, ordering or lifecycle capabilities based on validated requirements.

### Workflow Modernization
Replace fragile/manual orchestration with governed workflow where justified.

### Application / Experience Modernization
Implement validated seller, partner or operations experiences.

### Rules & Decision Modernization
Centralize or rationalize commercial/business decision logic when evidence supports fragmentation.

### Technical Debt Remediation
Address maintainability/reliability issues supported by code/platform evidence.

## Delivery Sequence
Validated requirement → architecture/design → implementation → automated/functional validation → operational readiness → measured outcome.

## Expected Outcomes
Reliable supported capability, clearer ownership, reduced workaround dependence, maintainable implementation and improved change readiness.

## Guardrail
Do not prescribe rebuild, migration or custom engineering when configuration/process change may solve the evidenced need.
