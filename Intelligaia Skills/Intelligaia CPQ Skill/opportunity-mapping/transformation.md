# CPQ/Q2C Transformation Opportunities

## Use When
Evidence shows the issue spans process, organization, governance, experience and technology rather than a single feature.

## Typical Evidence Signals
- fragmented ownership across quote-to-cash;
- inconsistent processes/business rules across teams or channels;
- multiple modernization initiatives without shared target state;
- platform overlap or unclear responsibility;
- backlog dominated by local fixes to systemic problems;
- lifecycle/billing issues caused by upstream process/data decisions;
- weak governance for commercial rules and change.

## Service Motions
### Q2C Target Operating Model
Clarify process ownership, decision rights, governance, platform responsibilities and operational measures.

### Future-State Journey & Architecture
Align user journeys, capabilities, systems, data/state and integration boundaries.

### Transformation Roadmap
Sequence discovery, UX, platform, integration, data and change initiatives around business dependencies.

### Governance & Product Model
Establish ownership for commercial rules, backlog, architecture, UX, releases and outcome measurement.

### Change / Adoption Enablement
Support role/process transition after the future state is validated.

## Expected Outcomes
Shared target state, clearer accountability, sequenced investment, reduced cross-team ambiguity and measurable transformation governance.

## Guardrail
Do not label a problem “transformation” merely to enlarge the engagement. Use this motion only when evidence shows systemic cross-domain change.
