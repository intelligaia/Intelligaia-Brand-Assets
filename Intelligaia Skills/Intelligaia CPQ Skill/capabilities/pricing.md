# Pricing

## Capability taxonomy
- List/base price and price lists
- Attribute-based pricing
- Tiered pricing
- Ramp pricing
- Customer/contractual pricing
- Channel pricing
- Deal-registration discount
- Promotions
- Discretionary/manual discounts
- Renewal uplift/pricing
- Price waterfall
- Multi-currency
- Pricing APIs/integration where validated

## Diagnostic questions
What is the price source? Which dimensions affect price? Which adjustments are automatic? What requires justification/approval? Can sellers explain the waterfall? How are future ramps and renewal pricing represented? How is pricing reconciled downstream?

## Outcomes
Pricing and packaging agility, profitability visibility, deal guidance, faster approvals, and scalable governance.
