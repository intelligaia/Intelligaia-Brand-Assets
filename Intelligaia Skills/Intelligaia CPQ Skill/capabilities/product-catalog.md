# Product Catalog

## Objective
Define and govern products/SKUs, attributes, lifecycle status, bundles, price-list eligibility, and selling context.

## Assess
- User steps and manual work
- Rules and exceptions
- Required data
- Approval/control points
- Upstream/downstream dependencies
- Error/rework patterns
- UX friction
- KPIs

## Desired outcome
A scalable, understandable capability that preserves commercial accuracy and reduces unnecessary touch.
