# Persona — Customer / Buyer

## Purpose
Represent the external party evaluating, negotiating, accepting, purchasing, and receiving the commercial offer.

# Customer / Buyer

## Objective
Understand the proposed solution and commercial commitment, obtain acceptable terms, complete purchasing requirements, and receive what was agreed.

## Lifecycle Touchpoints
Discovery/solution discussion → Proposal → Negotiation → Contract/Acceptance → Purchase Order where applicable → Fulfillment/Provisioning → Billing → Amendment/Renewal.

## Key Tasks
- review products/services and quantities;
- understand price/discount/term;
- compare scenarios;
- request changes;
- negotiate commercial/legal terms;
- provide purchasing/billing information;
- approve/sign;
- provide PO where required;
- receive/access service;
- review invoices;
- participate in renewal/change.

## Information Needed
Clear scope, configuration, price, discount where appropriate, term, dates, contractual obligations, billing/payment conditions, implementation/fulfillment expectations.

## Decisions
Accept/reject; request product/quantity/term/price change; request legal changes; select scenario; renew/expand/reduce/cancel according to rights and policy.

## Common Handoffs
Seller, Legal/procurement, finance/AP, implementation/operations, customer success.

## Experience Needs
- understandable proposal;
- consistent commercial information across documents;
- transparent next steps;
- low-friction signature/purchasing;
- accurate invoice matching;
- clear change/renewal process.

## Q2C Implications
Customer changes can trigger internal reconfiguration, repricing, reapproval, contract regeneration, order correction, or billing changes.

## Diagnostic Questions
Which customer requests cause the most internal rework? Are proposals easy to understand? Do contract/order/invoice values remain consistent? How are customer changes communicated and tracked?

## Evidence Rule
Buyer organization, procurement process, approval authority, and preferred experience are customer-specific.
