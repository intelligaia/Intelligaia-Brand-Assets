# Personas — Revenue & Commercial Operations

## Purpose
Define operational personas that govern process, catalog, ordering, billing, and fulfillment.

# 1. Sales Operations / Revenue Operations

## Objective
Create a scalable, measurable commercial operating model across people, process, systems, and data.

## Responsibilities
Process governance, policy operationalization, data quality, reporting, adoption, workflow design, release/change coordination, KPI visibility.

## Information Needed
Lifecycle metrics, process exceptions, data quality, system usage, seller/Deal Desk support patterns, integration failures, backlog/change demand.

## UX Needs
Reliable reporting, auditable process state, clear ownership, configurable governance, low manual reconciliation.

## Diagnostic Questions
Who owns end-to-end process? Which metrics are trusted? Where do teams use offline workarounds? Who owns policy vs system implementation?

# 2. Product / Catalog Operations

## Objective
Ensure products/services are commercially ready, discoverable, configurable, priceable, and governable.

## Responsibilities
Catalog maintenance, product lifecycle, attributes/options/bundles, eligibility, commercial metadata, coordination of configuration/pricing readiness.

## Information Needed
Product definitions, launch/end-of-sale dates, market/channel eligibility, dependencies, pricing readiness, downstream identifiers.

## UX Needs
Controlled maintenance, validation before release, dependency visibility, effective dating, auditability.

## Example KPIs
Catalog change lead time, catalog defects, configuration errors caused by catalog data, launch readiness.

# 3. Order Operations

## Objective
Convert accepted commercial transactions into accurate executable orders and resolve exceptions.

## Responsibilities
Order validation, downstream-required data, booking/submission, exception correction, quote/order reconciliation.

## Information Needed
Accepted quote, products/configuration, price/discount, dates, customer parties, PO, payment/billing context, fulfillment/entitlement data, downstream validation errors.

## UX Needs
Quote/order comparison, actionable errors, source-of-truth visibility, minimal re-entry, retry/reconciliation status.

## Example KPIs
Manual order touch, order cycle, rejection rate, auto-book rate, quote/order discrepancy.

# 4. Billing Operations

## Objective
Ensure commercial/order terms are translated into accurate billing execution.

## Responsibilities
Billing account/context, charge setup, billing exceptions, invoice-related correction, upstream/downstream reconciliation.

## Information Needed
Customer/billing account, order, charges, price/discount, quantities, dates, billing frequency, payment terms, subscription state.

## UX Needs
Traceability to commercial source, clear exception reason, reliable synchronization, correction/reconciliation workflow.

## Example KPIs
Billing errors, manual reconciliation, time to bill, upstream-data-related disputes.

# 5. Fulfillment / Provisioning

## Objective
Deliver or activate what the customer purchased.

## Responsibilities
Receive executable order, fulfill/provision, establish entitlement/asset/service state, report status/errors.

## Information Needed
Product/configuration, quantity, customer/entitlement context, dates, fulfillment instructions.

## UX Needs
Complete order data, clear dependencies, status/error propagation, correlation to originating order.

## Example KPIs
Activation/fulfillment time, failure rate, manual correction.

## Evidence Rule
Operational ownership and system boundaries vary significantly by company and must be validated.
