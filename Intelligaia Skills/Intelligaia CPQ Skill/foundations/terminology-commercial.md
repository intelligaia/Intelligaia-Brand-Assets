# Terminology — Quote, Approval, Contract & Order

## Quote
A structured commercial record containing proposed products/services, quantities, prices, discounts, dates, terms, and related customer/deal context.

## Quote Header
Quote-level information applying to the transaction, such as customer, currency, dates, status, and commercial context.

## Quote Line
A line-level representation of a quoted product/service/charge and its configuration/pricing context.

## Quote Version / Revision
A changed iteration of a quote. Versioning behavior varies by implementation.

## Quote Scenario
An alternative commercial configuration/pricing proposal evaluated for the same selling context.

## Primary / Authoritative Quote
The quote designated to drive the relevant opportunity/order/downstream commercial state. Exact platform terminology varies.

## Budgetary / Indicative Quote
A non-final commercial estimate used for planning or early customer discussion.

## Proposal
A customer-facing commercial document generated from quote/commercial data.

## Order Form
A customer-facing/executable commercial document used to capture agreed products/services and terms. Relationship to contract/agreement varies by organization.

## Approval
An authorized business decision on a transaction or exception.

## Approval Trigger
A rule/condition that causes approval to be required.

## Approval Matrix
The rules defining which approver(s) are required under which conditions.

## Approver
A person/group authorized to make an approval decision.

## Sequential Approval
Approval in which one approval must occur before the next.

## Parallel Approval
Multiple approvals that can occur concurrently.

## Group Approval
Approval assigned to a group/queue according to implementation policy.

## Delegation
Temporary or defined reassignment of approval responsibility.

## Escalation
Routing or notification used when an approval requires higher authority or exceeds an expected condition/SLA.

## Reject
Approval decision that sends the transaction back for revision/correction.

## Need More Information
Approval response requesting additional context before a final decision.

## Reapproval
A new approval cycle triggered after a material change to an already approved transaction.

## Approval Audit Trail
Recorded history of approval routing, decisions, comments, timing, and actors.

## Contract
A legally binding agreement governing the commercial relationship/transaction.

## Agreement
A broad term for documented terms accepted by parties. It may be synonymous with or broader than contract depending on the organization.

## Standard Terms
Pre-approved contractual/commercial terms.

## Non-Standard Terms
Terms that deviate from standard policy/template and may require Legal or other approval.

## Clause
A defined provision within an agreement.

## Redline
A proposed change to contractual language during negotiation.

## CLM
Contract Lifecycle Management: capabilities/processes used to create, review, negotiate, approve, execute, store, and manage agreements.

## eSignature
Electronic execution/acceptance of a document.

## Order
An executable record representing accepted commercial intent for downstream processing.

## Order Header
Order-level information applying to the transaction.

## Order Line
A product/service/charge line submitted for downstream execution.

## Order Validation
Checks that an order contains valid and complete information before submission/booking.

## Order Booking
Acceptance of an order into the organization's execution/order-management process. Exact definition varies.

## Auto-Book
Automated order booking when defined validation/control conditions are satisfied.

## Purchase Order (PO)
A customer-issued purchasing reference/document. Whether it is mandatory depends on policy/customer context.

## Sold-to
Party/entity to which the commercial sale is attributed.

## Bill-to
Party/entity responsible for billing.

## Ship-to
Party/location receiving physical delivery.

## Entitle-to
Party/entity receiving entitlement/access. Exact use is implementation-specific.

## Quote-to-Order Fidelity
The degree to which the accepted quote's products, configuration, quantity, price, discount, dates, and terms are preserved in the order.
