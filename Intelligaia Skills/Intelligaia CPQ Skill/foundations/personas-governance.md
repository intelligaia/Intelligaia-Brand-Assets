# Personas — Commercial Governance

## Purpose
Define roles that govern complex deals, pricing, approvals, and contractual exceptions.

# 1. Deal Desk

## Objective
Enable commercially sound deals while enforcing policy and reducing avoidable complexity for sellers.

## Responsibilities
Complex configuration support, pricing/discount exceptions, quote quality, deal structure, approval readiness, exception coordination, process guidance.

## Key Tasks
- review non-standard deals;
- troubleshoot configuration/pricing;
- assess discount/term exceptions;
- improve approval context;
- coordinate Finance/Legal/management;
- help resolve quote/order issues where commercial context is involved.

## Information Needed
Full deal context, customer history, configuration, price waterfall, requested exception, justification, terms, approval history, comparable policy context where governed.

## UX Needs
Consolidated deal view, exception highlighting, explainable calculations, efficient bulk review, comments/history, clear ownership and status.

## Example KPIs
Deal Desk touch rate, handling time, repeat/rework, approval readiness, seller escalations.

## Diagnostic Questions
Which standard transactions still require Deal Desk? What information is missing at intake? Which issues repeat? Is Deal Desk correcting data or making commercial decisions?

# 2. Pricing / Finance

## Objective
Protect pricing/commercial policy and financial quality while enabling appropriate deal flexibility.

## Responsibilities
Pricing policy, discount thresholds, exception review, financial/economic review where applicable.

## Information Needed
List/base/net price, adjustments, quantity, term, customer context, cost/margin only where relevant/available, justification, precedent/policy.

## Decisions
Approve/reject/request information; define policy/rules; determine whether exception is acceptable.

## UX Needs
Clear price derivation, exception magnitude, business justification, comparable context where authorized, audit history.

## Example KPIs
Exception volume, approval turnaround, discount leakage where measured, repeated manual pricing exceptions.

# 3. Approver / Management

## Objective
Make timely, informed decisions on transactions requiring authority.

## Key Tasks
Review context, understand exception, approve/reject/request more information, provide comments, escalate/delegate where applicable.

## Information Needed
Customer/deal summary, commercial value, exception reason, requested vs policy position, prior approvals/comments, downstream implication.

## UX Needs
Concise decision context, mobile/efficient review where appropriate, clear action, SLA/status, no need to reconstruct the deal manually.

## Example KPIs
Approval turnaround, aged approvals, rejection/more-information rate, reapproval.

# 4. Legal

## Objective
Protect contractual/legal position while enabling efficient execution of standard and non-standard business.

## Responsibilities
Non-standard terms, clauses, agreement review, negotiation/redlines, legal approvals, contract execution support.

## Information Needed
Approved commercial proposal, standard template/clauses, requested deviations, customer redlines, commercial impact of negotiated changes.

## Critical Handoff
If negotiation changes product, quantity, price, discount, term, or other governed commercial condition, the change may need to return to quote/pricing/approval.

## UX Needs
Traceable agreement versions, clause context, clear commercial linkage, review/approval status, reduced copy/paste between commercial and contract systems.

## Example KPIs
Legal touch rate, contract cycle, redline iterations, commercial rework caused by negotiation.

## Evidence Rule
Approval authority, thresholds, Legal involvement, Deal Desk scope, and Finance responsibilities are customer-specific and must be validated.
