# Terminology — Subscription, Amendment, Renewal & Billing

## Subscription
A time-based ongoing commercial/service relationship for a product/service.

## Subscription Term
The defined duration of a subscription relationship.

## Start / Effective Date
The date on which a commercial relationship, charge, product, or change becomes effective.

## End Date
The date on which the current term/service period ends.

## Renewal Date
The date around which continuation/renewal is evaluated or becomes effective.

## Evergreen / Auto-Renew
A relationship that continues automatically according to agreed terms unless changed/terminated. Exact rules require contract/policy evidence.

## Asset
A representation of something purchased, owned, installed, licensed, or entitled to a customer. Meaning varies by platform.

## Installed Base
The collection of active/historical products, subscriptions, assets, or entitlements associated with a customer.

## Entitlement
A right to use/access/receive a product, service, support, or capability.

## Amendment
An in-term change to an active subscription/contract/commercial relationship.

## Add-On
An additional product/service added to an existing relationship.

## Expansion
An increase in commercial scope, such as quantity, capacity, products, or value.

## Upsell
Moving a customer toward a higher-value product/tier/quantity.

## Cross-Sell
Selling an additional related product/service.

## Upgrade
Moving from an existing offering/configuration to a higher/newer offering according to applicable rules.

## Downgrade
Moving to a lower/reduced offering/configuration according to applicable rules.

## Quantity Increase / Reduction
An in-term change to quantity.

## Co-term
Aligning multiple subscription/product end dates to a common renewal date.

## Proration
Financial calculation for a partial period caused by mid-cycle start/change/end.

## Renewal
Continuation/restructuring of an expiring relationship for a subsequent term.

## Renewal Opportunity
A sales record representing a renewal selling motion. Creation/ownership varies by implementation.

## Renewal Quote
A quote used to propose continuation/change of an existing relationship.

## Renewal Uplift
A price increase applied at renewal according to policy/contract.

## Partial Renewal
Renewing only part of the expiring products/services/quantities.

## Non-Renewal
Decision not to continue an expiring relationship.

## Cancellation / Termination
Ending an active relationship according to applicable policy/contract.

## Billing Account
The billing-specific customer/account context used to manage charges, invoices, payment terms, and related financial activity.

## Charge
A billable monetary component associated with a product/service.

## One-Time Charge
A charge billed once.

## Recurring Charge
A charge billed repeatedly according to a frequency.

## Usage Charge
A charge calculated from measured consumption.

## Billing Frequency
How often recurring billing occurs.

## Billing Period
The time interval represented by a billing event/invoice.

## Invoice
A financial document requesting payment for billed charges.

## Credit
A reduction/reversal of an amount owed, subject to financial policy.

## Payment Terms
Agreed conditions defining when/how invoiced amounts are due.

## Accounts Receivable (AR)
Amounts owed by customers after invoicing.

## Collection
Activities/processes used to obtain payment of receivables.

## Revenue
Financial value recognized/reported according to accounting policy. Do not infer revenue-recognition treatment from CPQ data alone.

## Billing Reconciliation
Comparing upstream commercial/order/subscription state with billing state to identify and correct discrepancies.
