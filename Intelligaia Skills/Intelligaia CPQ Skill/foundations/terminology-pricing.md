# Terminology — Pricing & Discounting

## Price
The monetary commercial value assigned to a product/service under a defined context.

## Price List
A governed set of prices applicable to products/services for a market, currency, channel, customer type, or other context.

## Base Price
A starting/reference price used by a pricing calculation.

## List Price
A published/reference commercial price before applicable adjustments. Some implementations use Base and List differently.

## Unit Price
Price per unit before multiplication by quantity.

## Extended Price
Price calculated across quantity, commonly unit price × quantity before/after specified adjustments depending on model.

## Net Price
The resulting price after applicable adjustments/discounts according to the pricing model.

## Price Waterfall
A representation of how price changes from a starting value through adjustments/discounts to the resulting commercial price.

## Price Rule
Logic that calculates, changes, selects, or influences price based on commercial context.

## Attribute-Based Pricing
Pricing influenced by configuration attributes.

## Quantity / Volume Pricing
Pricing influenced by purchased quantity.

## Tiered Pricing
Pricing in which defined quantity/usage/value bands determine price.

## Term-Based Pricing
Pricing influenced by contract/subscription duration.

## Ramp Pricing
Pricing that changes across defined periods of a commercial term.

## Contractual / Customer-Specific Pricing
Pricing negotiated or defined for a specific customer/contract relationship.

## Channel Pricing
Pricing or adjustments based on partner/channel context.

## Renewal Pricing
Pricing applied to renewal transactions, potentially using existing price, uplift, index, policy, or new commercial terms.

## Uplift
An increase applied to an existing/reference price, often associated with renewal or future periods.

## Promotion
A governed commercial incentive available when defined eligibility conditions are met.

## Discount
A reduction from a reference price.

## Standard Discount
A discount allowed under established policy/rules without exceptional governance.

## Discretionary / Non-Standard Discount
A seller-requested or exception discount outside standard automatic policy, commonly subject to justification/approval.

## Discount Percentage
Discount expressed as a percentage of a defined reference price.

## Discount Amount
Discount expressed as a monetary value.

## Discount Reason
The business justification/category associated with a discount.

## Price Adjustment
A broader modification to price that can be positive or negative.

## Override
A manual or rule-authorized replacement of a calculated/default commercial value.

## Floor Price
A minimum permitted price according to policy. Exact definition and enforcement are organization-specific.

## Margin
The difference/relationship between revenue/price and cost under a defined financial model. Do not assume a specific margin formula without evidence.

## Approval Threshold
A defined condition at which a pricing/discount/commercial exception requires authorization.

## Pricing Exception
A commercial condition outside standard pricing policy requiring additional handling or approval.

## Repricing / Recalculation
Re-running pricing logic after a relevant configuration, quantity, term, discount, customer, or other input changes.

## Proration
Calculating a partial-period financial amount when a charge/change applies for only part of a billing/service period.

## One-Time Charge
A non-recurring charge applied once according to agreed conditions.

## Recurring Charge
A charge that repeats according to a defined frequency/term.

## Usage-Based Charge
A charge based on measured consumption/usage.

## Minimum Commitment
A contractual/commercial minimum quantity, spend, usage, or value obligation. Exact behavior is implementation-specific.
