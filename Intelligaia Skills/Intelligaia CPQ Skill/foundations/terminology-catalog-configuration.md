# Terminology — Product Catalog & Configuration

## Catalog
The governed collection of products, services, bundles, options, attributes, commercial metadata, and related rules available to selling processes.

## Product
A business-defined item or service that can participate in a commercial offering.

## Service
A sellable activity/capability delivered to a customer, potentially one-time, recurring, usage-based, or otherwise structured.

## SKU
A stock/sellable identifier used to distinguish a product or commercial unit. SKU architecture is organization-specific.

## Offer / Offering
A packaged commercial proposition presented to a customer. It may combine products/services, pricing, eligibility, terms, and channel/market context.

## Bundle
A grouping of products/services/options sold or configured together.

## Parent / Child
A relationship in which one catalog/configuration item contains, governs, or contextualizes another.

## Option
A selectable component or choice within a product/bundle configuration.

## Attribute
A characteristic used to describe or configure a product/service, such as capacity, region, edition, or term-related selection.

## Attribute Value
A permitted value assigned to an attribute.

## Product Family
A logical grouping of related products. The exact effect on selling/pricing/reporting depends on implementation.

## Product Hierarchy
A structured relationship among catalog entities used for organization, inheritance, navigation, reporting, or rules.

## Catalog Eligibility
Rules determining whether a product/offer can be sold in a given customer, market, channel, date, or commercial context.

## Product Visibility
Rules determining whether a user can discover/select an item. Visibility and eligibility are related but not necessarily identical.

## Product Lifecycle
The states through which a product moves, such as planned, active/sellable, restricted, retired, or end-of-sale. Exact statuses are company-specific.

## Effective Dating
Applying start/end dates to products, prices, rules, terms, or commercial relationships.

## Configuration
The process/result of selecting products, options, attributes, quantities, terms, and dependencies to create a valid solution.

## Guided Selling
Question- or context-driven assistance that helps a seller/customer identify suitable products or configurations.

## Configuration Rule
Logic that governs valid product combinations, attributes, quantities, or other configuration behavior.

## Compatibility Rule
Logic determining whether selected items can coexist.

## Dependency Rule
Logic requiring one item/condition when another item/condition is selected.

## Exclusion Rule
Logic preventing a combination or selection.

## Inclusion Rule
Logic automatically or conditionally including a component.

## Required Option
An option that must be selected or present for a valid configuration.

## Optional Option
An option that can be selected but is not required.

## Cardinality
Rules controlling minimum/maximum selections or quantities within a configuration.

## Constraint
A condition limiting possible configuration choices.

## Validation
A check that configuration/data satisfies defined rules before progressing.

## Valid Configuration
A configuration that satisfies applicable product, option, attribute, compatibility, dependency, quantity, and other rules.

## Configuration Error
A failed validation condition requiring correction.

## Existing Asset / Installed Base
A representation of products/services already purchased, installed, entitled, or active for a customer. Exact semantics depend on the system.

## Entitlement
The customer's right to access, use, receive, or consume a product/service/support capability.

## Co-term
Aligning the end/renewal dates of multiple products/subscriptions to a common date.

## Short Co-term
A shorter initial term used to align an added item with an existing renewal date.

## Forward Co-term
Extending/aliging terms toward a future common date. Exact commercial treatment must be validated.
