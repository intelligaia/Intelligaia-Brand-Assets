# Personas — Selling Roles

## Purpose
Define vendor-neutral selling personas participating in CPQ/Q2C.

# 1. Account Executive / Seller

## Objective
Convert customer need into an accurate, competitive, governable commercial proposal and progress it to acceptance/order with minimal avoidable friction.

## Lifecycle Responsibilities
Opportunity → Configure → Price → Quote → Approval → Proposal/Contract → Order handoff.

## Key Tasks
- establish customer/deal context;
- create/reopen quote;
- discover/select products;
- configure solution;
- review pricing;
- request discounts/exceptions;
- provide approval justification;
- generate/present proposal;
- respond to customer changes;
- coordinate contract/order readiness.

## Key Decisions
Which solution/scenario to propose; quantity/term; whether to request an exception; which quote/scenario to advance; when customer changes require revision.

## Information Needed
Customer context, catalog, compatibility, price explanation, discount policy, approval status, terms, quote history, customer changes, downstream status.

## Common Handoffs
Product Specialist, Deal Desk, Pricing/Finance, Approver, Legal, Order Ops, Customer.

## UX Needs
Guided workflow, strong search, explainable configuration/pricing, early validation, visible approval status, easy revisions, preserved context, minimal duplicate entry.

## Example KPIs
Quote cycle time, seller touches, self-service, quote revisions, conversion, order errors attributable to quote preparation.

# 2. Partner / Channel Seller

## Objective
Create valid commercial transactions within partner eligibility, program, pricing, authorization, and channel rules.

## Key Tasks
Customer/deal context, product selection/configuration, partner-specific pricing/discounts, deal registration where applicable, quote/proposal, approval, order submission.

## Information Needed
Partner eligibility, product authorization, channel price, discounts/incentives, deal-registration status, customer/end-user context, order requirements.

## Common Handoffs
Channel team, Deal Desk, Pricing, internal approvers, customer, order operations.

## UX Needs
Clear partner-specific eligibility, transparent status, low dependence on internal experts, actionable validation, minimal re-entry across partner/internal systems.

## Diagnostic Questions
What can partners self-serve? Which tasks require internal intervention? Are price/eligibility rules visible? Where is partner/end-customer data re-entered?

# 3. Solution / Product Specialist

## Objective
Ensure the proposed solution is technically/commercially appropriate and configuration-valid.

## Key Tasks
Product discovery, architecture/solution guidance, complex configuration, dependency validation, alternative solution design.

## Information Needed
Customer requirements, catalog, product attributes, compatibility/dependency rules, lifecycle/availability, existing environment.

## Common Handoffs
Seller, Product/Catalog Ops, Deal Desk.

## UX Needs
Rich product context, fast comparison, configuration validation, visibility into dependencies and lifecycle status.

# 4. Customer Success / Renewal Seller

## Objective
Manage in-life commercial change, expansion, and renewal using accurate customer purchase/subscription context.

## Lifecycle Responsibilities
Installed base → Amendment/Expansion → Renewal → Non-renewal/Change.

## Key Tasks
Identify active/expiring relationships, review history, create renewal/amendment quote, add expansion, evaluate co-term, coordinate approvals/contracting, close lifecycle transaction.

## Information Needed
Active products/subscriptions/assets, quantities, dates, historical price, contract terms, usage/adoption where relevant, renewal policy, customer contacts.

## Common Handoffs
Seller/Account team, Deal Desk, Pricing, Legal, Billing/Ops, Customer.

## UX Needs
Single view of active relationship, reliable renewal population, historical commercial context, clear effective dates, guided amendment/renewal choices.

## Example KPIs
Renewal preparation time, manual touches, renewal cycle, expansion value where measured, renewal rate, lifecycle error/reconciliation.

## Evidence Rule
Do not assume seller/renewal ownership or compensation/process design without customer evidence.
