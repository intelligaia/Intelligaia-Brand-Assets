## Lifecycle Guardrail

**Authoritative Current State → Pending/Future State → Requested Delta → Effective Date → Commercial Consequence → Approval/Acceptance → Processing → Resulting State → Billing/Service Consequence**

Do not infer amendment, renewal, cancellation, billing or proration policy from UX/advisory recommendations.

## Duplicate-Source Rule
Duplicate copies of the same deck/readout do not count as independent corroboration unless material version differences are established.

## Quantified-Value Rule
Every metric must retain source context, population, period and whether it is baseline, estimate, target or measured result. Do not generalize proposal/sample/case-study numbers.
