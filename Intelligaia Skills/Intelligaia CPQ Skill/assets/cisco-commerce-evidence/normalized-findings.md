# Cisco Commerce — Normalized Evidence

## Case-Study Evidence Rules
Preserve the source's distinction among:
**context/challenge → research/assessment → proposed design → implemented solution → reported outcome**.

Do not collapse a case-study narrative into universal Cisco Commerce product behavior.

## Normalized Claim Classes

### `CISCO-FIND-001` — Commerce / CPQ experience friction
**Evidence state:** Validated within case-study scope when explicitly reported.
**Use:** UX, journey and analysis evidence.
**Guardrail:** Do not generalize to all CCW users or current releases.

### `CISCO-FIND-002` — Complex seller / partner workflow
**Evidence state:** Validated within source scope when documented.
**Use:** Partner/channel and workflow analysis.
**Guardrail:** Preserve the actor, transaction and timeframe from the source.

### `CISCO-FIND-003` — Cross-system / process handoff complexity
**Evidence state:** Validated within case-study scope when documented.
**Use:** Integration and end-to-end journey analysis.
**Guardrail:** Do not infer APIs, source-of-truth or technical root cause unless the source states them.

### `CISCO-DESIGN-001` — Experience / workflow redesign
**Evidence state:** Proposed or Implemented, depending on the exact source statement.
**Rule:** Design screenshots/concepts alone do not prove production implementation.

### `CISCO-DESIGN-002` — Commerce modernization / CommerceX direction
**Evidence state:** Proposed / strategic unless implementation evidence is explicit.
**Rule:** Executive presentation content must retain whether it is roadmap, concept, recommendation or delivered capability.

### `CISCO-OUTCOME-001` — Reported project outcome
**Evidence state:** Validated only as a reported case-study result.
**Rule:** Retain metric definition, population, period and source wording. Never generalize the result to another customer/project.

## Platform-Specific Guardrails
- Do not invent CCW objects, APIs, status values, rules or approval behavior.
- Do not treat a designed UI pattern as a native Cisco Commerce capability.
- Do not treat a case-study implementation as product-wide behavior.
- Do not treat executive roadmap language as delivered functionality.
- Preserve partner/distributor/customer roles when the source distinguishes them.

## Duplicate Handling
The two Cisco Commerce Case Study PDFs are treated as one evidence lineage until a version/content comparison proves otherwise. Duplicate copies cannot be counted as corroboration.

## Measured vs Claimed Outcomes
Classify every outcome as one of:
- measured result;
- reported result without measurement detail;
- target;
- estimate;
- qualitative outcome;
- proposed benefit.

Only measured/reported results can be described as source-reported outcomes, and only within the source scope.
