# Comparative Evidence — Validation Questions

1. What source company/project/timeframe does the reference claim apply to?
2. Is the source a case study, proposal, resource plan, research artifact or executive concept?
3. Was the referenced solution proposed, prototyped, piloted or implemented?
4. Is an outcome measured, merely reported, estimated, targeted or qualitative?
5. What actor/transaction/platform context produced the result?
6. Does independent target evidence establish a comparable problem?
7. Are platform/configuration and commercial-policy assumptions comparable?
8. Are integrations and authoritative systems comparable?
9. Is the target operating model/channel structure comparable?
10. What target-specific evidence is needed before recommending a similar approach?
11. What target-specific baseline is required before estimating value?
12. Could the reference pattern create confirmation bias or prematurely narrow discovery?
