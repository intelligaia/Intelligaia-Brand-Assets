# Proposal and Service Evidence

## Proposal Rule
A proposal or resource plan can establish that a service, workstream, role mix, phase or deliverable was **proposed**. It does not establish approval, execution, adoption or outcome.

## `PROP-EVID-001` — Advisory / Assessment
Forsys proposal material may inform reusable advisory workstream patterns. Customer need/readiness must be independently established.

## `PROP-EVID-002` — UX / Experience Work
UX/ecommerce proposal or case material may inform research/design methods. A proposed artifact or screen is not proof of production implementation.

## `PROP-EVID-003` — Resource / Delivery Planning
Resource plans may evidence proposed staffing/effort structure within their source context. Do not convert them into standard staffing ratios or effort estimates for another engagement.

## `PROP-EVID-004` — Modernization / Transformation
Executive presentations can evidence strategic direction, proposed modernization themes or conceptual architecture. Separate roadmap from delivered capability.

## Opportunity Mapping Guardrail
Use:
**target evidence → target pain/opportunity → applicable service motion → evidence-backed rationale**

Do not use:
**service catalog → infer target pain → manufacture value proposition**.
