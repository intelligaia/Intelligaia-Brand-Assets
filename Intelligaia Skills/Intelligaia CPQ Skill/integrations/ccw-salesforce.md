# Ccw Salesforce

## Purpose
Describe validated cross-system ownership, handoffs, mappings, and failure behavior.

## Capture
- Source and target
- Trigger/event
- System of record by entity
- Data payload / key fields
- ID mapping
- Direction and timing
- Transformations
- Validation
- Retry/idempotency
- Error ownership and reconciliation
- Monitoring/audit
- Security/permissions

> Do not invent APIs, endpoints, field mappings, or integration direction.
