# Story Analysis Framework

For each story assess:
1. Persona and business outcome
2. Trigger/preconditions
3. Functional behavior
4. Data inputs/outputs
5. Business rules
6. Platform ownership
7. Integrations/dependencies
8. Exceptions/error states
9. UX implications
10. Acceptance criteria
11. KPI/business impact
12. Open questions

Flag ambiguity, hidden dependencies, duplicated stories, conflicting rules, and missing non-functional needs.
