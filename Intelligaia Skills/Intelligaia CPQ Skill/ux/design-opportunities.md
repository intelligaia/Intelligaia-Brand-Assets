# CPQ Design Opportunities

## Purpose
Translate evidence-backed CPQ/Q2C usability findings into outcome-oriented future-state opportunities without prematurely prescribing screens, features, or platform implementations.

## Opportunity Chain
**Evidence → Finding → Root Cause → Desired Outcome → Design Principle → Opportunity → Dependencies → Validation → Requirement**

An opportunity is not automatically a feature request.

## Opportunity Areas
1. Context and transaction continuity
2. Guided product discovery and configuration
3. Commercial comprehension
4. Approval efficiency and transparency
5. Quote/version confidence
6. Customer handoff
7. Downstream status and recovery
8. Lifecycle change comprehension
9. Partner/channel continuity
10. Operations and exception management
11. Expert efficiency
12. Accessibility and role fit

## Evidence Rule
Current-state problems must be supported by evidence. Future-state ideas must be labeled **Proposed** until validated. Do not present a proposed UI, automation, AI behavior, integration, or workflow as an existing capability.
