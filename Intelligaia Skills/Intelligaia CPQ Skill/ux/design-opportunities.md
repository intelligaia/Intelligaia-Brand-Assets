# Design Opportunities

## CPQ UX lens
Evaluate task clarity, cognitive load, guidance, visibility of system status, error prevention/recovery, cross-tool continuity, contextual help, approval transparency, comparison, and speed for frequent tasks.

## Evidence capture
- Persona/task
- Observed friction
- Cause
- Business impact
- Design opportunity
- Validation method/KPI

Favor simplification and contextual guidance over exposing underlying CPQ complexity.
