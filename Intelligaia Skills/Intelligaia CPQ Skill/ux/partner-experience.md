# CPQ Partner Experience

## Purpose
Define the partner-facing CPQ/Q2C experience for channel selling while preserving end-customer, partner, distributor, payer, commercial and lifecycle context.

## Partner Outcome
A partner should be able to answer:
**Which customer/deal am I working on? → What am I authorized to sell? → What commercial context applies? → What must I provide? → What requires vendor action/approval? → What can I present to the customer? → What happens after acceptance?**

## Core Journey
**Partner Identity → Customer / Deal → Eligibility → Quote → Configure → Price → Discount / Deal Support → Approval → Customer Proposal → Acceptance → Order / Handoff → Track → Amend / Renew**

## Principles
- Preserve partner and end-customer roles separately.
- Make eligibility and authorization understandable.
- Minimize duplicate customer/deal entry.
- Protect confidential commercial information by role.
- Explain vendor/partner handoffs and ownership.
- Preserve quote/version identity across portals/systems.
- Support lifecycle continuity, not only initial sale.
- Make exceptions actionable without exposing internal-only data.
