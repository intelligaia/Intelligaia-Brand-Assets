# Partner Experience — Order & Lifecycle Continuity

## 1. Order Readiness
Before partner submission, show unresolved configuration, commercial, approval, customer and required-data blockers.

## 2. Duplicate Prevention
Repeated submission or uncertain outcome should not create duplicate business orders.

## 3. Processing State
Distinguish submitted, processing, partial, completed and blocked states at the appropriate business level.

## 4. Order Visibility
Provide partner-visible references/status needed for customer follow-up without exposing internal operational detail.

## 5. Partial Completion
If applicable, show affected products/components and whether partner action is required.

## 6. Failure
Explain:
affected transaction → preserved work → partner-correctable issue vs vendor/distributor issue → safe next action → support reference.

## 7. Customer Continuity
Partner should not need to recreate customer and installed-product context after initial sale.

## 8. Installed / Subscription State
For lifecycle work, show the authoritative subset needed for the partner's permitted actions.

## 9. Amendment
Represent:
current state → partner-requested change → effective date → resulting state → approval/commercial consequence.

## 10. Renewal
Represent:
current term → renewal boundary → eligible products/changes → next-term proposal → approval → acceptance → resulting state.

## 11. Pending Changes
Show relevant pending amendments/cancellations before another lifecycle transaction is initiated.

## 12. Partner Eligibility at Renewal
Validate current partner/customer relationship and renewal eligibility rather than assuming historic ownership.

## 13. Partner Change
If another partner takes over, preserve historical attribution while applying current authorization and commercial rules.

## 14. Distributor Continuity
Where a distributor remains part of lifecycle transactions, preserve the correct channel chain.

## 15. Cancellation / Non-Renewal
Expose only permitted actions and distinguish immediate change from end-of-term intent.

## 16. Billing / Service Visibility
Expose only decision-relevant billing/service state appropriate to the partner role and authorization.

## 17. Cross-System Continuity
Preserve business references and return paths when partner work moves among portal, CPQ, vendor, distributor or lifecycle systems.

## 18. History
Provide meaningful channel transaction history for support and renewal continuity where authorized.

## Evaluation Questions
- Can partners track an accepted quote after submission?
- Can they distinguish pending from completed?
- Can they start an amendment from current state?
- Are pending changes visible?
- Is current renewal ownership validated?
- Does a partner/distributor change break lifecycle traceability?

## Guardrail
Do not invent order visibility, subscription access, partner reassignment, renewal ownership or billing/service permissions.
