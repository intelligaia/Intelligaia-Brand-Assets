# Design Opportunity Framing

## 1. Start with Outcome
Frame opportunities around what should become easier, safer, clearer, or faster—not around a predetermined component.

Prefer:
“Help sellers resume the correct active commercial transaction.”

Avoid:
“Add a Resume button.”

## 2. Opportunity Statement
**How might we help [role] achieve [goal] in [context] while preserving [business/integrity requirement]?**

## 3. Evidence Link
Every opportunity should reference one or more findings or explicit business requirements.

If no evidence exists, label the opportunity exploratory.

## 4. Root-Cause Fit
Confirm the proposed direction addresses the validated/hypothesized cause.

A navigation redesign cannot fix an incorrect pricing rule.

## 5. Context Continuity
Potential outcome:
reduce repeated customer/deal/quote search and preserve transaction/version context across systems.

## 6. Guided Configuration
Potential outcome:
help users make valid product choices earlier and understand dependencies without exposing irrelevant complexity.

## 7. Commercial Comprehension
Potential outcome:
make price, discount, term, quantity, recurring/one-time value and material changes understandable.

## 8. Approval Readiness
Potential outcome:
show approval-driving conditions before submission and preserve exact version applicability.

## 9. Quote Confidence
Potential outcome:
make current, draft, approved, accepted, ordered and superseded commercial versions distinguishable.

## 10. Downstream Confidence
Potential outcome:
make submitted, processing, partial, completed and actionable exception state visible without integration-log knowledge.

## 11. Safe Recovery
Potential outcome:
preserve valid work and prevent duplicate business outcomes during errors/timeouts.

## 12. Lifecycle Clarity
Potential outcome:
show current baseline → requested delta → resulting state → effective date → supported commercial consequence.

## 13. Renewal Readiness
Potential outcome:
bring current term, pending changes, next-term decisions and renewal status into one coherent seller/partner task context.

## 14. Partner Continuity
Potential outcome:
preserve partner, distributor, end-customer and commercial-role context across quote/order/lifecycle work.

## 15. Operations Visibility
Potential outcome:
help operations identify affected transaction/component, owner, recovery path and reconciliation state.

## 16. Expert Efficiency
Potential outcome:
reduce repetitive low-value interactions while preserving validation and audit.

## 17. Accessibility
Potential outcome:
ensure core tasks and errors are understandable and operable across supported accessibility needs.

## 18. Automation
Automation opportunities should specify:
trigger → authoritative input → business rule → action → exception → audit → human override/approval where needed.

Do not automate ambiguity.

## 19. AI-Assisted Experiences
If proposed, define:
user goal → approved data context → recommendation/generation scope → explainability → user confirmation → policy/security boundaries → fallback.

AI should not silently commit commercial or lifecycle actions.

## 20. Cross-System Opportunity
Decide whether the improvement belongs in:
interaction layer → orchestration → integration → data → policy → platform → operating model.

## Guardrail
Do not convert a preferred solution into a “user need” or claim expected impact without validation.
