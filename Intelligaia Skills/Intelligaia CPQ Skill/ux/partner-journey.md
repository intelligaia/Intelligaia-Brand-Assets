# Partner Experience — Journey & Context

## 1. Partner Identity
Establish authenticated partner organization/user, authorized role and applicable selling context.

## 2. Customer Identity
Keep partner identity distinct from end customer, sold-to, ship-to/service-to and payer/bill-to roles.

## 3. Customer Search / Creation
Where permitted:
find existing customer → resolve match → create/request new customer only when needed → prevent duplicates.

## 4. Deal Context
Partner may enter from registered deal, opportunity, quote, renewal or customer context. Preserve the governing business reference.

## 5. Eligibility
Before configuration, determine whether the partner/customer/product/region/channel combination is eligible according to authoritative rules.

## 6. Start vs Resume
Check for existing quote/deal work before creating another transaction.

## 7. Product Discovery
Show products/solutions available to that partner/customer context without exposing unauthorized catalog or internal-only data.

## 8. Configure
Guide required options and dependencies with partner-appropriate terminology.

## 9. Commercial Context
Make clear which commercial values the partner can see, request or change and which are vendor/system controlled.

## 10. Deal Support
If special pricing/deal registration/discount support exists, show required inputs, current status and relationship to the quote.

Exact program mechanics require evidence.

## 11. Approval
Partner should understand whether action is pending with partner, distributor or vendor at a business level supported by evidence.

## 12. Customer Proposal
Customer-facing output should preserve the correct partner/end-customer context and governed quote/version.

## 13. Acceptance
Capture/correlate accepted version according to the approved channel process.

## 14. Order
Preserve partner/customer/quote identity into ordering and show business-relevant status.

## 15. Distributor Handoff
If a distributor participates, distinguish distributor from partner and customer and preserve transaction correlation.

## 16. Fulfillment / Subscription
Expose only downstream state needed for partner/customer follow-up.

## 17. Amendment
Start from authoritative installed/subscription state and preserve original/current channel relationships as required.

## 18. Renewal
Identify the renewing customer/subscription/term and applicable partner ownership/eligibility rather than assuming the original partner always owns renewal.

## 19. Ownership Change
If partner relationship changes, use approved reassignment/transfer rules and preserve history.

## 20. Support
Errors should tell the partner what they can correct and when vendor/distributor action is required.

## Diagnostic Questions
- Where are partner and end-customer identities conflated?
- Where is customer/deal data re-entered?
- Can partners create duplicate quotes?
- Are eligibility failures explained?
- Is special-pricing/approval status understandable?
- Does the partner lose visibility after order submission?
- Can renewals identify the correct partner relationship?

## Guardrail
Do not invent channel programs, eligibility, ownership, distributor mechanics or observed partner pain points.
