# Cross-Platform Partner & Channel Stories

## Outcome
Preserve party identity, authorization and commercial visibility across partner-assisted Q2C transactions.

## Party Model
Where evidenced distinguish:
seller → partner → distributor → end customer → sold-to → bill-to/payer → service recipient.

## Story Pattern
**As a** `[actor/role evidenced for this scenario]`
**When** `[business trigger/context, if evidenced and useful]`
**I want** `[specific need/action/capability derived from evidence]`
**So that** `[specific business outcome derived from the actor, decision/action, and desired consequence; otherwise Validation Needed]`

## Flow
1. Actor and partner/customer relationship are established.
2. Authorization and visibility are validated.
3. Commercial transaction carries the correct party context.
4. Pricing/approval uses the appropriate evidenced context.
5. Order handoff preserves relevant party identities.
6. Subscription/billing/service state applies to the intended parties.
7. Resulting references/status remain traceable.

## Decisions
Who may quote? → who sees commercial values? → who approves? → who orders? → who pays? → who receives service? → who owns lifecycle interaction?

## Risks
party-role collapse → wrong customer → unauthorized commercial visibility → partner lost during handoff → distributor mismatch → renewal ownership mismatch.

## Acceptance Focus
identity fidelity, authorization, confidentiality, downstream party mapping and lifecycle continuity.

## Guardrail
Do not invent partner-program, distributor, pricing, territory or ownership rules.
