# CCW Partner / Channel Story Patterns

## Purpose
Capture channel-selling outcomes without collapsing partner, distributor and end-customer identities.

## Role Model
Preserve evidenced distinctions among:
seller → partner → distributor → end customer → payer → service recipient → approver.

Do not assume all roles exist in every transaction.

## Story Pattern — Partner Quote
Capture:
authorized partner → intended customer → eligible transaction → commercial visibility → quote ownership/context → governed proposal.

## Story Pattern — Partner Commercial Access
Capture:
what the partner may view/change → what is restricted → customer/confidential commercial information → authorization basis.

## Story Pattern — Distributor Handoff
If evidenced:
partner/customer relationship → distributor role → transaction handoff → commercial/order identity → resulting status.

## Special Pricing / Deal Context
Preserve source terminology and validate:
eligibility → commercial authority → approval → version → customer/partner association.

## Lifecycle Ownership
For renewals/amendments, validate whether partner ownership/eligibility changes and how customer/subscription identity is preserved.

## Identity Integrity Questions
- Who is selling?
- Who is buying?
- Who pays?
- Who receives service?
- Who owns the quote/deal relationship?
- Who may see which commercial values?
- Which identity must survive into order/lifecycle systems?
