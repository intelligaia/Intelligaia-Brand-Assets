# Intelligaia Opportunity Mapping

## Commercial chain
Evidence → CPQ Pain Point → Business Impact → Needed Capability → Intelligaia Service → Deliverable → KPI/Outcome.

## Service motions supported by supplied material
- CPQ / Q2C assessment and advisory
- User research and contextual interviews
- Journey mapping and service blueprinting
- Jobs-to-be-done / persona workshops
- UX strategy and heuristic/usability review
- Information architecture
- Rapid prototyping / MVP-in-a-Week style design workshops
- High-fidelity UI and interaction design
- Design systems and component libraries
- Salesforce/CPQ transformation support
- Data/integration rationalization
- User-story and roadmap definition
- Product/application implementation support

## Example mapping
**Evidence:** sellers rely on Deal Desk for standard proposal generation.  
**Impact:** slower response and Deal Desk overload.  
**Needed capability:** governed self-service proposal generation.  
**Possible engagement:** workflow/UX assessment + future-state design + story definition + implementation support.  
**KPIs:** quote cycle time, Deal Desk touch rate, self-service rate.

Never invent ROI or claim a service fit without evidence.
