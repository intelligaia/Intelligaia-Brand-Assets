CX Strategy
Competitive Analysis
Benchmarking of competitors and category leaders across key experience dimensions.
Five competitors were evaluated across six dimensions: onboarding, feature discoverability, self-serve support, mobile experience, personalization, and accessibility. The analysis revealed a significant gap in guided onboarding and contextual help — most competitors rely on static documentation rather than in-product guidance.

Customer Journey Mapping
End-to-end journey stages, touchpoints, emotions, and pain points for the primary persona.
Sarah's journey was mapped across six stages: Awareness, Sign-up, Onboarding, First Value, Ongoing Use, and Advocacy. The biggest drop-off occurs during Onboarding (42% churn) and the emotional low-point is at "First Value" where users report feeling overwhelmed by feature breadth without contextual guidance.

Service Blueprint
Frontstage customer actions, backstage processes, supporting systems, and the gaps between them.
The blueprint covers the onboarding flow end-to-end. Key gap identified: the frontstage "guided setup" wizard is not connected to the backstage user-profile API, causing personalization to fail silently. Three backstage processes (notification service, analytics pipeline, and feature-flag system) need integration to support the proposed progressive onboarding experience.

Design System
