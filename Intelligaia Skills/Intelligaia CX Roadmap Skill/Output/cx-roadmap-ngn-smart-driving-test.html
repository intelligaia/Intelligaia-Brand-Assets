CX Roadmap

Discovery Workshop

Independent review of NGN's current Smart Driving Test platform to pinpoint the root causes behind the four PoC blockers.

It Includes:
  • AI does one job todayA YOLO-family model detects and segments the vehicle each frame. All violation decisions — including line-touch — are deterministic geometry and rules.
  • Wheels are inferred, not seenRoot cause behind unreliable line-touch detection and the reason direct contact-point detection is proposed.
  • Engine-stall path is incompleteCriterion is present but never populated, and OBD is mocked — a contained, well-defined fix.
  • Four PoC focus areas identifiedLine-touch, camera angle, camera placement, engine stall — the levers that matter for measurable accuracy.

Business Objectives

Demonstrate, on NGN's real test footage and setup, that the four focus areas can be solved reliably and measurably — no more, no less.

Four-wheel line-touch detection
Per-wheel touch/cross detection with measured precision & recall against a labelled clip set.
Optimal camera angle
A documented recommended angle range, backed by a side-by-side accuracy comparison.
Camera placement plan
Positions, mounting height, lens/FOV and camera count that meet coverage and resolution targets.
Engine stall detection
Reliable stall detection via real OBD telemetry (optionally corroborated by audio/motion), validated against ground truth.
Measured accuracy & go/no-go
A concise PoC evaluation report with a clear go/no-go recommendation for any larger programme.
4–6 week focused engagement
Small team (one CV/ML engineer + part-time lead), delivered in three stages: scoping → build → validation.
