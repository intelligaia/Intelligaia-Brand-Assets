Business Objectives
The measurable outcomes the Urban Mobility platform must deliver — from PoC to city-scale.
Automate on-street parking end-to-end
Detect vacancy, read plates, track dwell, calculate fees, and flag violations from existing CCTV — no manual intervention.
Reach production-grade plate accuracy
Lift number-plate reading above the "unknown" threshold on blurred, distant, and angled plates using local dataset fine-tuning and a VLM fallback.
Scale beyond one site and two cameras
Prove the pipeline holds across multiple cameras and sites — distributed inference, queued processing, and defined GPU economics.
Deliver a citizen-facing experience
Ship the driver & fee-collector experience — mobile app or WhatsApp/Telegram — so drivers can start, pay, and contest sessions without leaving the flow.
Enable live digital payments
Wire a bank/RMA gateway plus a parking wallet so notified bills are payable in the step they arrive — wallet, QR checkout, or collector counter.
Establish the Smart City foundation
Position parking as phase one of a scalable urban mobility platform — traffic analytics, road safety, and a command centre sequenced after the pilot.

Customer Journey Mapping
Where the driver & officer journeys break today — from arrival to session-close, notice, and resolution.
Journey moment Gap in the connected experience Suggested approach
Arrival & session startNo single way in. On-street parking has no gate; a session begins only when the camera reads a plate. A driver with no app, or whose plate is misread, cannot start the session or see it running. Channel-independent session start: ANPR default · printed bay QR opens a no-app web session · SMS short code keyed to bay number. De-dup rules merge claims into the open ANPR session.
Bay-type recognitionOn-street bays mix free, paid, time-limited and exempt vehicles. Each type needs the right on-screen state, or users are billed or flagged incorrectly. Model policy as data (tariff, free/paid, time limit, exemptions) — effective-dated so tariffs change without redeploy. Billing reads the bay's active rule + vehicle exemption from BCTA.
Driver identityThe plate identifies the owner, not the driver — family, pooled, and fleet vehicles are driven by different people. Billing or penalising the registered owner for someone else's session invites disputes. Separate vehicle identity from responsible user. Bay QR scan binds session to the actual driver's account and payment method; standing delegation for fleet accounts; full audit trail.
Reaching the userNo defined channel confirms a session, bill or violation; the mobile app doesn't exist. The experience cannot be completed or even communicated. Channel-abstraction layer behind the notification agent with fallback order: SMS baseline · bay QR/web · mobile app + WhatsApp/Telegram as upgrades. Typed messages, delivery receipts, retries.
PaymentBills are recorded but there's no path the driver can finish. A recorded bill that cannot be paid is not a working loop. Payment-service layer wiring local gateway/PSP + parking wallet: auto-deduction at close · QR checkout for one-offs · collector counter for cash. Daily reconciliation against the billing ledger.
Enforcement & disputeA driver cannot see, pay, or contest a violation. Enforcement without a notice-and-dispute path erodes trust and is unenforceable in practice. Closed loop: VLM-verified evidence pack → notice with unique ref → pay-or-contest → officer queue (approve/waive/uphold) → policy-driven escalation. Every step logged.
Unreadable platesBlurred, distant, angled, or dirty plates return "unknown". Session cannot be attributed or charged at all. Layer identification: multi-frame voting · confidence-gated VLM second read · super-resolution on small crops · optional RFID fallback in high-value zones. Persist "occupied-but-unidentified" state.
Coverage gapsCameras don't yet cover every billable bay. Partial coverage shows phantom availability and misses sessions. Decouple the two camera jobs: cheap wide-angle for occupancy across many bays + fewer high-res/PTZ for plate reading at entries. Per-site coverage map before go-live; "coverage gap" state — not "available" — for unobserved bays.
