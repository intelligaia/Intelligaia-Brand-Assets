Principles

How Intelligaia design decides.

Three principles govern every visual and functional call. When two are in tension, resolve toward the one earlier in this list.

Goodness leads

Design serves the person on the other side, not the ego of the maker. Utility, dignity, delight — in that order.

Intent-aware

Every element earns its place by serving the user's intent right now — not a hypothetical future need.

Human-centered

Machines assist, but humans decide. Interfaces stay legible, reversible and honest about what's automated.

Typography

Two families. One rhythm.

Oswald sets the display voice — condensed, editorial, confident. Geist carries the reading load — neutral, technical, honest. Nothing else.

Display · Oswald 400 / 500
Aa
Every H1, H2 and headline. Medium (500) for signature titles, Regular (400) for sub-heads. No other weights.
Body · Geist 400
Aa
All body copy, ledes, descriptions, labels, buttons, nav and meta — plus Geist Mono. 400 for running text, 500 for micro-labels. Never italic.
