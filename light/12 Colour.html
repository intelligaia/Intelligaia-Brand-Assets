Design System / Colour

Colour.

30 locked tokens across six groups — brand hero, primary, secondary, base, skin and hair — plus 13 gradient paint styles. Every colour used at Intelligaia comes from this list.

Use only these 4 for anything customer-facing

Primary brand palette.

Every marketing artifact — landing pages, decks, one-pagers, social posts — uses these four colours. Nothing else. The wider illustration palette lives in Assets and stays inside illustration files.

Usage rules

How to use each colour.

YellowOnly for CTAs, brand-hero banners and the brand mark. Never fills more than 15% of any surface (except the yellow hero banner itself).Max 15% BlackAll body copy, headings, borders, dark backgrounds. The default ink of the system.Unlimited BlueText links, info states, secondary interactive elements. Not decorative.As needed PurpleSecondary accent — badges, small illustrations, brand-hero moments. Never for text.Max 10%
Guardrails

DO / DON'T.

DO

  • Use only the 4 primary colours in customer-facing artifacts
  • Keep yellow scarce — it earns attention because it's rare
  • Reserve black for text and chrome
  • Use blue for links and info states only

DON'T

  • Use more than 2 non-neutral colours in a single artifact
  • Recolour illustrations to match a client palette
  • Use purple for body text (contrast + brand consistency)
  • Pull skin/hair/gradient tokens into marketing artifacts
