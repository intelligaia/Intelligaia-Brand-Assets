Service catalog

Templates clients keep.

The service catalog template — six reusable page types, one grid, assembled without a designer.

Purpose

The document a client keeps.

A service catalog is the tangible artifact of a pitch — capabilities, proof and value in a form someone can forward internally. Every page in this template obeys the same grid so an agent can assemble one without a designer.

Format

A4 landscape

Print-safe margins, screen-legible at 50% zoom.

Grid

12 column

88px gutter. Content never breaks the outer margin.

Cover

Yellow banner

Signature treatment. Client name, date, one line.

Length

8–14 pages

Beyond that it stops being read.

Template pages

Six page types, reusable.

Assemble a catalog by picking pages and swapping content. Order is flexible after the cover.

Cover

Yellow banner, title, client mark.

About

Who we are — one page, three proof points.

Capabilities

Service grid with icons and one-line descriptions.

Differentiators

What we do best — three columns.

Business value

Client-specific value mapping.

Case study spread

Problem, approach, artifacts, outcome.
Assembly

How to build one.

Set the cover

Yellow banner, client name, engagement title, date. One line of positioning.

Page 1
Establish credibility

About page — who Intelligaia is, in three proof points, not three paragraphs.

Page 2
Show the capability surface

Capability grid. Icons from the library, one line each, no marketing adjectives.

Pages 3–4
Differentiate

What we do best. Three columns, each with a concrete example rather than a claim.

Page 5
Map value to this client

Client-specific. Their problem, our service, the measurable outcome.

Pages 6–8
Prove it

One or two case study spreads. Real artifacts, real numbers.

Pages 9+
Rules

Do & don't.

Do

  • One idea per page — if it needs two, it needs two pages
  • Every claim paired with an artifact or a number
  • Illustrations from the approved library only
  • Client name on the cover and in the value section

Don't

  • No stock photography, ever
  • No page over 120 words of body copy
  • No gradient on the cover banner
  • No generic capability lists without client mapping
