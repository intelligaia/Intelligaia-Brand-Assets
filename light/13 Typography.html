DO / DON'T.

DO

  • Use Oswald for every heading, Geist for every body
  • Reserve Oswald Bold 700 for the landing H1 only
  • Stick to the seven-step scale — don't invent sizes
  • Let line-height do the work of spacing between paragraphs

DON'T

  • Introduce a third font family (no Fraunces, no Inter)
  • Italicise headings or bold body copy for emphasis
  • Letter-space Geist body — it kills readability
  • Use Silkscreen anywhere in new artifacts
