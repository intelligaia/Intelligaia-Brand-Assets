Whitepapers

Design and systems, paired.

The engineering whitepaper format — two tracks addressed together, never in separate halves.

Purpose

Two tracks, one document.

An engineering whitepaper has to satisfy two readers at once — the design lead who wants to know why the interface behaves that way, and the architect who wants to know what it costs to run. Pair them explicitly rather than alternating chapters and hoping.

Design track
  • User model and mental model gap
  • Interface behaviour and states
  • Information hierarchy decisions
  • Accessibility and failure states
Engineering track
  • Architecture and data flow
  • Latency, cost and scale characteristics
  • Integration surface and contracts
  • Observability and failure recovery
Structure

Seven sections, paired throughout.

Each section addresses both tracks before moving on. Never a design half and an engineering half.

Abstract

What the product does, who it's for, what's technically distinctive. 150 words.

1 page
The problem domain

Why this is hard. Both the user-side difficulty and the systems-side difficulty.

2 pages
Approach

The design principle and the architectural principle, stated as a pair.

2 pages
The system

Architecture diagram, data flow, component responsibilities. Design annotations inline.

3–5 pages
The interface

Key screens with behaviour specs. Each paired with the technical constraint that shaped it.

3–5 pages
Results

Performance numbers, adoption, measured design outcomes. Honest about what wasn't measured.

2 pages
What we'd change

The section that earns trust. Real limitations, real next steps.

Rules

Do & don't.

Do

  • Pair every design claim with its engineering consequence
  • Include one architecture diagram using the Diagrams playbook
  • State latency and cost in real units
  • Write a genuine limitations section

Don't

  • No marketing language in a technical document
  • No architecture diagram without design annotation
  • No benchmark without stating the conditions
  • No claim of novelty without a citation or comparison
